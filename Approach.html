We invest in businesses capable of building globally scalable African intellectual property. Every investment is assessed through our proprietary framework, designed to identify scalable creative businesses with strong IP, commercial potential and long-term value creation.

03 · Outcome

Built to endure.

Every company we back receives access to the Dunia Value Creation Studio — our post-investment platform designed to accelerate growth, reduce execution risk and strengthen long-term enterprise value.

Creative businesses fail less often because of talent shortages than because of weak IP structures, poor distribution, governance gaps and limited access to growth capital. The Dunia Value Creation Studio is designed to address exactly those challenges.

From selection to scale, we partner with founders to build enduring African creative-economy leaders.
